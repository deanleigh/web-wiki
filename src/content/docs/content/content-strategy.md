---
title: Content Strategy
description: A practical frame for deciding what content to create, improve, and retire.
---

Content strategy sits under Content and answers why content exists, who it serves, and what outcomes it should drive.

## Core questions

- Which audiences are primary right now?
- Which tasks or decisions must content support?
- Which formats and channels are most effective?
- Which content should be created, refreshed, merged, or retired?

## Done when

- Priorities are documented by audience and outcome.
- A content roadmap exists with owners and dates.
