---
title: Governance, Ownership and Compliance
description: A simple operating model for keeping website content accurate, safe, and accountable.
---

This page combines the three areas that often break first when ownership is unclear.

## Governance

- Define publishing standards and quality thresholds.
- Set review cadence for priority sections.
- Track exceptions and unresolved risks.

## Ownership

- Assign an accountable owner per section.
- Define who can draft, review, approve, and publish.
- Define escalation path for urgent corrections.

## Compliance

- Map legal or regulatory requirements that affect content.
- Maintain records for consent, claims, and policy-driven statements.
- Include compliance checks in pre-publish workflow.

## Done when

- Named owners exist for each section.
- Governance rules are documented and actively used.
- Compliance checks are repeatable and auditable.
