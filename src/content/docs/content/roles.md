---
title: Content Roles
description: The people who plan, write, edit, and govern the content on a website.
---

Content is a craft with named specialists. Most organisations need more than one of these roles, even if one person sometimes wears several hats.

- [Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)](/roles/cmo/) — senior executive responsible for marketing strategy.
- [Head of Marketing](/roles/head-of-marketing/) — leads the marketing team day-to-day.
- [Content Strategist](/roles/content-strategist/) — decides what content the site needs and why.
- [Copywriter](/roles/copywriter/) — writes the words on the site.
- [Editor](/roles/editor/) — reviews copy for accuracy, tone, structure, and house style.
- [Proofreader](/roles/proofreader/) — final check for spelling, grammar, and punctuation.
- [Content Owner](/roles/content-owner/) — accountable for a section staying accurate and current.
- [Subject Matter Expert (SME)](/roles/subject-matter-expert/) — provides accurate domain knowledge.
- [Translator](/roles/translator/) — translates content from a source language to a target language.
