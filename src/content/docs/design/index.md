Design is more than the way a website looks. It covers visual identity, layout, interaction, and the consistency that makes a site feel coherent across every page and screen.

## What to agree first

- What does the brand stand for, and how should the site reflect that?
- Who are the primary audiences, and what do they expect visually?
- Which devices, screen sizes, and contexts must the design work in?
- Is there an existing design system, or does one need to be created?

## Minimum baseline

- A defined visual identity covering colour, type, spacing, and imagery.
- Consistent components reused across templates.
- Designs tested against real content, not placeholder text and stock images.
- Responsive behaviour considered from the start, not retrofitted.

## Process expectations

- Design works with content, not around it.
- Accessibility is part of the design process, not a later fix.
- Decisions are documented so future changes stay consistent.

## Done when

- Visual identity and component library are documented.
- Design decisions are recorded and accessible to anyone who needs them.
