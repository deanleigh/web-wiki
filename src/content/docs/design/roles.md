---
title: Design Roles
description: The people who shape how a website looks, feels, and behaves.
---

Design covers a wider range of roles than most clients realise. Visual design, interaction design, brand, and research are all distinct disciplines.

- [Graphic Designer](/roles/graphic-designer/) — creates visual assets, layouts, and finished page designs.
- [User Experience (UX) Designer](/roles/ux-designer/) — designs how the site works, maps journeys, prototypes interactions.
- [User Researcher](/roles/user-researcher/) — plans and runs research with real users.
- [Conversion Rate Optimisation (CRO) Specialist](/roles/cro-specialist/) — improves how well the site converts.
- [Brand and Identity Designer](/roles/brand-and-identity-designer/) — defines or maintains the visual identity.
- [Photographer](/roles/photographer/) — produces original photography for the site.
- [Image Librarian](/roles/image-librarian/) — manages the organisation's image assets.
- [Illustrator](/roles/illustrator/) — produces custom illustrations where photography is not the right fit.
- [Motion Designer](/roles/motion-designer/) — produces animated content and micro-interactions.
