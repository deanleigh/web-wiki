This is the practical hosting page for client conversations. Keep the focus on risk, ownership, and day-to-day realities.

## What to agree first

- Who owns the hosting account and billing?
- What uptime target is realistic for this site?
- Is there a staging environment before production?
- How are backups handled and tested?
- Who is responsible for incidents and out-of-hours problems?

## Minimum technical baseline

- SSL/TLS enabled and auto-renewing.
- CDN and caching configured.
- Access is role-based, not shared credentials.
- Server/platform updates are part of routine maintenance.
- Monitoring and alerting are in place.

## Common trade-offs to explain

- Lower cost usually means less support and slower incident handling.
- Simpler stacks are easier to maintain for small teams.
- Managed hosting can reduce operational burden.

## Done when

- Hosting owner is named.
- Support and incident process are documented.
- Backup and restore process is tested.
