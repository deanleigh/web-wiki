---
title: Hosting Roles
description: The people who keep a website running, online, and reachable.
---

Hosting roles are mostly technical, mostly invisible until something breaks. Knowing who does what makes incidents shorter and ownership clearer.

- [Chief Technology Officer (CTO)](/roles/cto/) — holds overall accountability for technology choices.
- [Database Administrator (DBA)](/roles/dba/) — owns the database structure, performance, backups, and access.
- [DevOps Engineer](/roles/devops-engineer/) — manages infrastructure, deployments, and monitoring.
- [Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)](/roles/site-reliability-engineer/) — focused on uptime, performance, and incident response.
- [Network Administrator](/roles/network-administrator/) — manages networking, DNS, and connectivity.
- [Hosting Account Owner](/roles/hosting-account-owner/) — holds commercial responsibility for the hosting account.
