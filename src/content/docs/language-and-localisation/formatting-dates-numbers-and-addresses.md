---
title: Formatting Dates, Numbers and Addresses
description: Presenting dates, numbers, currencies, and addresses in formats users recognise.
---

Locale shows up in small details. Dates, numbers, currencies, phone numbers, and addresses all carry expectations. Getting them wrong looks careless even when the words are correct.

## What to agree first

- Which date and time formats are expected by the audience?
- Which currency, decimal, and thousands separators are used?
- Which address and phone formats need to be supported?
- Are units of measure metric, imperial, or both?

## Minimum baseline

- Dates use an unambiguous format suited to the audience.
- Numbers and currencies follow locale conventions.
- Phone numbers are presented in their national format and stored in international form.
- Address fields and order match the country being served.

## Done when

- Formatting rules are documented per locale.
- Templates and components use those rules consistently.
