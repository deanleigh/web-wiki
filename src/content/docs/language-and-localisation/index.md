title: Language and Localisation
description: Editorial and technical decisions that shape how a website serves its audiences in their language and locale.
---

Language and localisation cuts across content, SEO, accessibility, and usability. It is both an editorial discipline and a technical one. This pillar keeps both in view.

## What belongs here

- Voice, tone, dialect, and spelling conventions.
- Technical declarations such as `lang` and `hreflang`.
- URL strategies for multilingual sites.
- Locale-aware formatting for dates, numbers, currencies, and addresses.
- Translation workflow and terminology management.

## Working principle

A site that hides its language choices guesses on behalf of its users. A site that declares them clearly serves users, search engines, and assistive technology at the same time.
