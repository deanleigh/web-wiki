---
title: Multilingual URLs
description: Choosing a URL strategy that signals language and region clearly to users and search engines.
---

URL structure is one of the first signals search engines and users use to identify a page's language and audience. Pick a strategy early and apply it consistently.

## What to agree first

- Which markets and languages does the site need to serve?
- Is content fully translated, partially translated, or regionally varied?
- Which strategy fits the business: country domains, subdomains, or subdirectories?

## Minimum baseline

- One strategy is chosen and applied consistently across the site.
- Each language or locale has stable, predictable URLs.
- The default locale is handled deliberately, not by accident.
- Language switching does not break deep links.

## Common trade-offs

- Country-code domains signal locale strongly but are operationally heavier.
- Subdirectories are simpler to host and easier to consolidate authority.
- Subdomains sit between the two but can fragment analytics.

## Done when

- URL strategy is documented and agreed.
- Default locale and fallback behaviour are defined.
