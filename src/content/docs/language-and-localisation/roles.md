---
title: Language and Localisation Roles
description: The people who shape how a site speaks to its audiences in their language and locale.
---

Language and localisation roles range from editorial to technical. Most projects need at least an owner, even if no translation is involved.

- [Style Guide Owner](/roles/style-guide-owner/) — owns the document that defines voice, tone, dialect, spelling, and terminology.
- [Translator](/roles/translator/) — translates content from a source language to a target language.
- [In-Country Reviewer](/roles/in-country-reviewer/) — reviews translations for accuracy, tone, and cultural fit.
- [Terminology Owner](/roles/terminology-owner/) — maintains the glossary or termbase of approved terms.
- [Localisation Project Manager](/roles/localisation-project-manager/) — coordinates translation, review, and integration across languages.
