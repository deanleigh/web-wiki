---
title: Voice, Tone and Spelling
description: Agreeing the editorial voice, dialect, and spelling conventions that define how a website sounds.
---

Voice and tone make a site feel coherent. Dialect and spelling decisions stop the same word being written three different ways across the site.

## What to agree first

- Which dialect of English (or other language) is the site written in?
- What is the brand voice, and how does tone shift by context?
- Which terminology is preferred, and which is to be avoided?
- Who owns the style guide and decides on changes?

## Minimum baseline

- A short style guide exists and is shared with anyone who writes for the site.
- Spelling, capitalisation, and punctuation rules are explicit.
- Common terms have a single agreed form.
- Brand and product names have a definitive spelling.

## Done when

- A style guide is published and referenced from editorial workflow.
- Authors and editors know where to check before publishing.
