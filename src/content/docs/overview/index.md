This wiki sits between the things you know about your business and the people who will help you build and run your website. Its job is to make sure nothing important gets missed.

## What you will find here

Each section covers a discipline that shapes how well a website serves the people who use it. Hosting, search, accessibility, usability, design, content, language, planning, and ownership all sit alongside each other. None of them stand alone.

## How to use it

Pages are short by design. Each one names a topic, frames the decisions and questions that matter, and points to specialists or further reading for anything deeper. Treat the "Done when" lists as conversation prompts with your team or supplier, not a tick-box exercise.

## How the sections relate

Most topics touch others. Accessibility, usability, and search rely on each other. Content depends on language. Hosting touches almost everything. Where one topic leans on another, you will see links between them. Follow them.

## Where it stops

This wiki will not turn you into a specialist. It will give you the language to ask the right questions, recognise good answers, and know when to bring in someone whose career is built on a single corner of one of these topics.
