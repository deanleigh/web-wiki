---
title: Planning and Project Management
description: Setting websites up for success before build work begins, and keeping them on track once it does.
---

Most website problems start before anyone writes code or designs a page. Unclear goals, missing owners, undefined scope, and unspoken assumptions cause the majority of avoidable rework.

## What belongs here

- Project scoping, goals, and success criteria.
- Stakeholders, decision-makers, and approval flow.
- Risk, dependencies, and assumptions.
- Delivery readiness and pre-launch checks.

## Working principle

A clear plan does not promise no surprises. It makes sure the surprises that arrive are the ones that could not reasonably have been spotted earlier.
