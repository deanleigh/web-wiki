---
title: Website Preflight Checklist
description: A practical checklist for scoping, planning, and de-risking a website before delivery work begins.
---

Use this checklist at the start of a project and again before build kickoff. The goal is simple: reduce surprises, define ownership, and make better decisions earlier.

## 1) Business goals and outcomes

- What is the primary purpose of the website right now?
- What does success look like in 3-6 months?
- Which outcomes matter most: leads, sales, support reduction, trust, recruitment, reputation?
- Which audience segments are highest priority?
- What should users do on key pages?

Done when:

- 1-3 measurable objectives are agreed.
- Primary user journeys are named.

## 2) Stakeholders and decision model

- Who is the final decision-maker?
- Who owns day-to-day website operations?
- Who signs off on copy, design, legal, and technical changes?
- Who can approve urgent fixes?
- How will disputes be resolved when priorities conflict?

Done when:

- Roles and responsibilities are written down.
- Approval process and SLAs are clear.

## 3) Information architecture and content

- Is there a current sitemap and does it reflect real user needs?
- Which pages are mandatory at launch?
- Which content is outdated, duplicated, or missing?
- Is there a content model for reusable blocks and structured data?
- Who writes, edits, checks facts, and publishes?

Done when:

- Launch sitemap is approved.
- Content owners and deadlines are assigned.

## 4) CMS and editorial workflow

- Which CMS is used and why is it fit for purpose?
- What roles are required (author, editor, publisher, admin)?
- Is there a draft-review-approve workflow?
- Is version history and rollback available?
- Are media standards in place (image sizes, naming, alt text, rights)?

Done when:

- Workflow states are documented.
- Editorial permissions map is agreed.

## 5) Domain, DNS, and hosting

- Who controls domain registrar access?
- Are DNS records documented and backed up?
- What environments exist (dev, staging, production)?
- Is SSL/TLS in place and auto-renewing?
- Is the hosting setup scalable for expected traffic?

Done when:

- Access ownership is confirmed.
- Environment and release path are documented.

## 6) Performance and Core Web Vitals

- What are target performance budgets (LCP, CLS, INP, page weight)?
- Are images optimised and responsive?
- Are scripts minimized, deferred, or removed where possible?
- Is caching configured for static and dynamic content?
- Are third-party scripts audited for value vs. cost?

Done when:

- Performance targets are explicit.
- Measurement plan and tools are agreed.

## 7) SEO and discoverability

- Is keyword intent mapped to priority pages?
- Are metadata standards defined (titles, descriptions, OG tags)?
- Are canonical URLs and redirects planned?
- Is technical indexing configured (sitemaps, robots, noindex rules)?
- Is structured data planned where useful?

Done when:

- SEO baseline requirements are documented.
- Ownership for ongoing SEO maintenance is assigned.

## 8) Analytics and reporting

- Which analytics platform(s) are used?
- Which events are essential to track?
- Are conversion goals and attribution expectations realistic?
- Are dashboards and reporting cadence defined?
- Are consent and privacy requirements reflected in tracking?

Done when:

- KPI dashboard requirements are agreed.
- Event tracking specification exists.

## 9) Accessibility and inclusion

- Is WCAG target level defined?
- Are templates designed for keyboard and screen-reader use?
- Are colour contrast and focus states tested?
- Is content guidance in place for readability and plain language?
- Is accessibility part of QA and release criteria?

Done when:

- Accessibility standards are part of definition of done.
- Testing responsibilities are assigned.

## 10) Security, privacy, and compliance

- Which regulations apply (for example GDPR/UK GDPR, PECR, sector rules)?
- Where is personal data collected and stored?
- Are forms protected against spam and abuse?
- Are patching, backups, and incident response defined?
- Is consent management configured and auditable?

Done when:

- Risk and compliance checklist is signed off.
- Security ownership and response process are clear.

## 11) Integrations and dependencies

- Which systems must integrate (CRM, ESP, payments, support, DAM, SSO)?
- What are the failure modes if an integration breaks?
- Are API limits, retries, and monitoring understood?
- Who owns each integration contract?
- Is there a fallback plan for critical integrations?

Done when:

- Integration inventory and owners are documented.
- Monitoring and fallback expectations are defined.

## 12) Operations, maintenance, and sustainability

- Who handles ongoing updates and content hygiene?
- Is there a maintenance cadence for plugins, dependencies, and audits?
- Is there an agreed change log and release note process?
- Are backups tested, not just scheduled?
- Are environmental impact goals considered (lighter pages, fewer unnecessary requests)?

Done when:

- Operational model is agreed post-launch.
- A 90-day improvement roadmap is defined.

## Suggested usage pattern

- Run a 60-90 minute preflight workshop.
- Mark each section as Green, Amber, or Red.
- Convert Amber/Red items into named actions with owners and dates.
- Revisit this checklist before launch and quarterly afterward.
