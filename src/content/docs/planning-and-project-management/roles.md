---
title: Planning and Project Management Roles
description: The people who plan, coordinate, and deliver a website project.
---

Most projects involve more roles than a client initially expects. Larger projects pull in multiple project and product managers across organisations.

- [Project Manager (Client Side)](/roles/project-manager-client/) — runs the project from the client's side, owning scope and internal stakeholders.
- [Project Manager (Agency Side)](/roles/project-manager-agency/) — runs delivery on behalf of the agency or supplier.
- [Product Owner (Client Side)](/roles/product-owner-client/) — owns what the product does and what success looks like.
- [Product Owner (Agency Side)](/roles/product-owner-agency/) — the agency's product lead, bridging design, technical, and content.
- [Business Analyst](/roles/business-analyst/) — translates business needs into requirements designers and developers can act on.
- [Key Stakeholder](/roles/key-stakeholder/) — named senior decision-maker consulted at specific points.
- [Sponsor](/roles/sponsor/) — funds the project and is accountable for its outcome.
