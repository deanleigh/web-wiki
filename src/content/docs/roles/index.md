Most website friction is unclear ownership. This pillar lists every role mentioned across the wiki in one place. Each pillar also has its own roles page picking out the roles most relevant to that discipline.

## Governance roles

These roles exist on every project regardless of discipline. They decide, sign off, and escalate.

- [Sponsor](/roles/sponsor/) — funds the project and is accountable for its outcome.
- [Key Stakeholder](/roles/key-stakeholder/) — named senior decision-maker consulted at specific points.
- [Project Manager (Client Side)](/roles/project-manager-client/) — runs the project from the client's side.
- [Project Manager (Agency Side)](/roles/project-manager-agency/) — runs delivery on behalf of the agency.
- [Product Owner (Client Side)](/roles/product-owner-client/) — owns what the product does, from the client's side.
- [Product Owner (Agency Side)](/roles/product-owner-agency/) — the agency's product lead.
- [Content Owner](/roles/content-owner/) — accountable for a section staying accurate and current.
- [Technical Owner](/roles/technical-owner/) — accountable for the platform, integrations, and incidents.
- [Compliance Owner](/roles/compliance-owner/) — oversees privacy, accessibility, and legal checks.

## Decision checkpoints

- Who approves urgent production fixes?
- Who can publish without additional sign-off?
- Who decides when priorities conflict?
- Who owns release scheduling?

## Workflow basics

- Draft -> Review -> Approve -> Publish.
- Named owner for each stage.
- Defined SLA for approvals.
- Rollback path for incorrect or risky changes.
