title: Security and Compliance
description: Protecting the site, its users, and the data it handles, and meeting the legal obligations that apply.
---

Security and compliance are usually invisible until they fail. Treat them as a discipline of their own, not a checklist bolted on at the end.

## What belongs here

- Hosting and platform security.
- Data protection and lifecycle.
- Authentication, authorisation, and access management.
- Legal and regulatory compliance.
- Security testing and audit.

## Working principle

Security cannot be added at the end. It is a thread that runs through every other discipline, from hosting choices to content workflows.
