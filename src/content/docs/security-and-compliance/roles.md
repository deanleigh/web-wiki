---
title: Security and Compliance Roles
description: The people involved in keeping a website secure and legally compliant.
---

Security and compliance pull in roles from both inside and outside your organisation. Most projects need a mix of legal, technical, and audit input.

- [Chief Technology Officer (CTO)](/roles/cto/) — holds technical accountability for security decisions.
- [Head of Legal and Compliance](/roles/head-of-legal-and-compliance/) — owns the legal view of the project.
- [Data Protection Officer (DPO)](/roles/data-protection-officer/) — specialist role for organisations under data protection law.
- [Penetration Tester](/roles/penetration-tester/) — attacks the site under controlled conditions and reports vulnerabilities.
- [Security Architect](/roles/security-architect/) — designs how systems fit together securely.
- [Compliance Auditor](/roles/compliance-auditor/) — reviews evidence that controls are in place and working.
- [Compliance Owner](/roles/compliance-owner/) — oversees privacy, accessibility, and legal checks across the project.
