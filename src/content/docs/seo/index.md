Treat SEO as an ongoing practice, not a launch task.

## What to agree first

- Which audience intents matter most?
- Which pages are priority landing pages?
- What counts as success: rankings, qualified traffic, conversions?
- Who owns SEO upkeep after launch?

## Minimum technical baseline

- Logical heading structure and clear page purpose.
- Unique page titles and meta descriptions.
- Canonical URLs and redirect rules.
- XML sitemap and robots strategy.
- Fast page performance and mobile-friendly layouts.

## Content expectations

- Every priority page answers a real user question.
- Internal links support key journeys.
- Content is reviewed and updated on a schedule.
- Image alt text supports accessibility and context.

## Done when

- Priority keywords and pages are mapped.
- Technical baseline is implemented.
- Ongoing ownership and reporting cadence are agreed.
