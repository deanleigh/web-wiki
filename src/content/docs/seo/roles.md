---
title: SEO Roles
description: The people who make a website discoverable and keep it that way.
---

SEO is more than one job. Technical, content, and analytical specialists each cover different ground.

- [SEO Team Leader](/roles/seo-team-leader/) — owns SEO strategy and ensures other disciplines support it.
- [Technical SEO Specialist](/roles/technical-seo-specialist/) — focuses on site structure, performance, indexing, schema, and redirects.
- [Content SEO Specialist](/roles/content-seo-specialist/) — aligns content with what audiences search for.
- [Link-Building Specialist](/roles/link-building-specialist/) — develops the site's authority through external links.
- [Analytics Consultant](/roles/analytics-consultant/) — configures tracking and translates data into recommendations.
- [Social Media Manager](/roles/social-media-manager/) — owns the social presence that supports SEO indirectly.
- [Marketing Manager](/roles/marketing-manager/) — coordinates marketing activity across channels.
