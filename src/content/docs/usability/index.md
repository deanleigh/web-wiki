---
title: Usability
description: A practical framework for making websites easier to understand and use.
---

Usability is about helping people complete tasks with minimum friction.

## What to agree first

- What are the top tasks users need to complete?
- What does a good journey look like from first visit to outcome?
- Which pages or steps are currently causing confusion?

## Minimum baseline

- Clear page purpose and plain language.
- Consistent navigation and link labelling.
- Strong visual hierarchy and scannable content.
- Predictable forms and feedback messages.
- Obvious calls to action with clear outcomes.

## Process expectations

- Test journeys with real users or internal proxies regularly.
- Use analytics and session evidence to spot friction.
- Prioritise changes that reduce task time and error rate.

## Done when

- Key journeys are documented and measured.
- Improvement backlog is active and reviewed regularly.
