---
title: Usability Roles
description: The people who make sure a website is easy to understand and use.
---

Usability shares roles with Design, Accessibility, and Content.

- [User Researcher](/roles/user-researcher/) — plans and runs research with real users.
- [User Experience (UX) Designer](/roles/ux-designer/) — designs the structure, flow, and interactions of the site.
- [Usability Tester](/roles/usability-tester/) — runs structured tests of specific tasks with real users.
- [Participant Recruiter](/roles/participant-recruiter/) — sources and books real users for research and testing.
- [Information Architect](/roles/information-architect/) — designs how content is organised, labelled, and navigated.
- [Accessibility Specialist](/roles/accessibility-specialist/) — reviews work against accessibility standards.
